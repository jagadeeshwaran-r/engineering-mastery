> Mathematical fluency

## Why this category exists

Software engineers use mathematics to reason precisely about quantities, relationships and change. This shelf establishes notation and algebraic intuition that later supports algorithms, statistics and AI.

## What you'll learn

- Manipulate algebraic expressions and functions
- Understand change through basic calculus
- Explain solutions with clear steps

## Books and why they belong here

| Book / guide | Role | Why it is included |
|---|---|---|
| [Basic Mathematics](../reading-guides/basic-mathematics.md) | Foundation | Rebuilds algebra, functions, and mathematical fluency. |
| [Calculus Made Easy](../reading-guides/calculus-made-easy.md) | Foundation | Makes change, derivatives, and integration approachable. |

## Suggested reading order

Start with **Basic Mathematics** for algebraic confidence, then use **Calculus Made Easy** for a first intuitive encounter with calculus.

## A practical outcome

Explain what a derivative means, solve elementary algebra problems, and show your working.
