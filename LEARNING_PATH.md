# Learning Paths

[← Home](README.md) · [Catalog](CATALOG.md) · [Start here](START_HERE.md)

These are **suggested routes**, not required courses or a recommendation to read every edition. Start with the subject that best matches your immediate learning goal.

## 1. Backend & systems

**For:** Java backend developers who want to understand implementation, concurrency, and architecture.

| Step | Study | Intended result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [The Pragmatic Programmer](reading-guides/the-pragmatic-programmer.md) | Develop durable work and learning habits |
| 2 | [Clean Code](reading-guides/clean-code.md) and [Clean Architecture](reading-guides/clean-architecture.md) | Refactor deliberately and explain boundaries |
| 3 | [Computer Systems: A Programmer's Perspective](reading-guides/computer-systems-a-programmers-perspective.md) | Understand what happens beneath the application |
| 4 | [Java Concurrency in Practice](reading-guides/java-concurrency-in-practice.md) | Reason about shared state and thread safety |
| 5 | [Designing Data-Intensive Applications](reading-guides/designing-data-intensive-applications.md) | Weigh storage and distributed-system trade-offs |

**Milestone:** design a small backend service and document one concurrency concern, one storage trade-off, and one refactoring decision.

## 2. Algorithms & reasoning

**For:** learners who want stronger mathematical and algorithmic problem solving.

| Step | Study | Intended result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Basic Mathematics](reading-guides/basic-mathematics.md) (as needed) | Refresh notation and algebra |
| 2 | [How to Prove It](reading-guides/how-to-prove-it.md) | Establish reasoning habits |
| 3 | [How to Solve It](reading-guides/how-to-solve-it.md) | Learn a repeatable problem-solving process |
| 4 | [Discrete Mathematics and Its Applications](reading-guides/discrete-mathematics.md) | Work with discrete structures |
| 5 | [Introduction to Algorithms](reading-guides/introduction-to-algorithms.md) | Analyze correctness and efficiency |
| 6 | [The Art and Craft of Problem Solving](reading-guides/art-and-craft-of-problem-solving.md) | Deepen problem-solving flexibility |

**Milestone:** solve and explain several problems, include correctness reasoning and implement at least one solution in Java.

## 3. Math to AI

**For:** readers preparing for future machine-learning study.

| Step | Study | Intended result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Basic Mathematics](reading-guides/basic-mathematics.md) | Revisit algebra and functions |
| 2 | [Calculus Made Easy](reading-guides/calculus-made-easy.md) | Build intuition for derivatives and change |
| 3 | [Logic & Discrete Mathematics](02_Logic_and_Discrete_Math/README.md) | Strengthen formal reasoning |
| 4 | **Prerequisites not yet represented:** linear algebra, probability, statistics and optimization | Identify which new references to curate |
| 5 | [Artificial Intelligence shelf](08_Artificial_Intelligence/README.md) | Add future AI references with documented learning goals |

**Milestone:** build a mathematical prerequisites checklist before selecting your first ML textbook.

## Study loop

**Understand → practice → explain → revisit.** After reading a section, produce an original example, verify assumptions, and record questions.

All reading statuses are currently **Not tracked**; there is no claim that these paths have been completed.
