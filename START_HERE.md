Welcome to **Engineering Mastery**. This is a guided map of technical books—not a claim that all titles have been read, reviewed cover-to-cover, or licensed for public redistribution.

## Three ways to explore

**By question:** Open the [annotated catalog](CATALOG.md), find a title and read its **why this book** explanation.

**By subject:** Open one of the numbered folders. Each folder explains the purpose of the topic, lists the books that belong there, and suggests a concrete learning outcome.

**By goal:** Follow [Backend & systems](LEARNING_PATH.md#1-backend--systems), [Algorithms & reasoning](LEARNING_PATH.md#2-algorithms--reasoning), or [Math to AI](LEARNING_PATH.md#3-math-to-ai).

## Understanding the labels

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Purpose** | The editorial reason this reference belongs in the collection |
| **Focus** | Concepts worth investigating while reading |
| **Practice** | An original exercise to apply what was learned |
| **Edition note** | Version information, including anything not yet confirmed |
| **Not tracked** | Reading progress was not provided; completion must not be assumed |

## How to use one book

1. Read the short rationale in its category page.
2. Open its companion guide under [reading-guides](reading-guides/README.md).
3. Define one objective (e.g., understand replication trade-offs or prove an invariant).
4. Read selectively and work through an example on your own.
5. Record a short original explanation, a practical application, and an open question.
