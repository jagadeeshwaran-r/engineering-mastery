Makes change, derivatives, and integration approachable. It supports the larger goal of mathematical fluency.

## What to look for

- Rates of change
- Derivatives
- Integrals
- Intuition

## How to approach it

Read with an application in mind. Write a brief original explanation of **rates of change, derivatives, integrals, intuition**, then test that understanding with the exercise below.

## Put it into practice

Derive and graph a simple function's rate of change; check the result numerically.
