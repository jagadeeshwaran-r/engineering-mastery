[← All guides](README.md) · [Catalog](../CATALOG.md) · [Logic & Discrete Mathematics](../02_Logic_and_Discrete_Math/README.md)

**Author:** Daniel J. Velleman

**Role in the library:** Reasoning

## Why this book is here

Teaches how to build and communicate sound proofs. It supports the larger goal of proofs and discrete structures.

## What to look for

- Statements
- Quantifiers
- Direct proofs
- Contradiction
- Induction

## How to approach it

Read with an application in mind. Write a brief original explanation of **statements, quantifiers, direct proofs, contradiction, induction**, then test that understanding with the exercise below.

## Put it into practice

Write a short proof, then identify its assumptions and conclusion.
