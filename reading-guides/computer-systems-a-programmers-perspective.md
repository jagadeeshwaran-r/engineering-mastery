# Computer Systems: A Programmer's Perspective

[← All guides](README.md) · [Catalog](../CATALOG.md) · [Systems & Architecture](../05_Systems_Architecture/README.md)

**Author:** Randal E. Bryant & David R. O’Hallaron

**Role in the library:** Systems foundations

## Why this book is here

Explains the machine-level behavior underlying software performance and correctness. It supports the larger goal of how software works at scale.

## What to look for

- Memory
- Machine representations
- Programs
- Linking
- System-level behavior

## How to approach it

Choose **one edition** as your primary systems reference; the second edition, third edition and separate 2016-named file may overlap. Compare editions only when a specific topic demands it.

## Put it into practice

Trace how a small program uses memory and identify what can affect performance.
