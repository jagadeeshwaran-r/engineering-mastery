[← Home](../README.md) · [Start here](../START_HERE.md) · [Catalog](../CATALOG.md) · [Reading paths](../LEARNING_PATH.md)

> Strategy before syntax

## Why this category exists

Knowing a programming language is not the same as knowing how to approach a new problem. These works emphasize heuristics, exploration and reflection.

## What you'll learn

- Reframe and break down problems
- Choose a strategy before coding
- Review and generalize a solution

## Books and why they belong here

| Book / guide | Role | Why it is included |
|---|---|---|
| [How to Solve It](../reading-guides/how-to-solve-it.md) | Method | Introduces a repeatable approach to unfamiliar problems. |
| [The Art and Craft of Problem Solving](../reading-guides/art-and-craft-of-problem-solving.md) | Deep practice | Develops deeper mathematical problem-solving techniques and persistence. |

## Suggested reading order

Begin with **How to Solve It**, then deepen your practice with **The Art and Craft of Problem Solving**.

## A practical outcome

Keep a log of assumptions, ideas tried, final solution and lessons learned for three unfamiliar problems.
