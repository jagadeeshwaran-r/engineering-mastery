# Content and Copyright Policy

This is a public **bibliography and learning-notes repository**, not an ebook file-sharing archive.

- Book titles, authors, and editions are listed for identification and reference.
- Do not upload copyrighted third-party textbooks, PDFs, scans, or reproduced chapters without explicit distribution rights.
- Original study notes and appropriately cited short quotations may be shared subject to applicable rights and law.
- Links to publishers, authors, and openly licensed material are welcome.
- The presence of a book in the catalog gives no right to redistribute it.

**Repository licensing:** No project-wide content license has been selected. Public visibility does not itself grant rights to reuse the notes. A license can be chosen separately later.
